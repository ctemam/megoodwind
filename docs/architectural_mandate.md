GOVERNING PRINCIPLE: research-driven, competition-aware, self-diagnosing, continuously
improving DeFi arbitrage/flash-loan system, grounded in authoritative evidence,
designed to evolve toward top-3 in its defined competitive category.

3 predictive intelligence (strategies, variants, regime, evidence-based selection)

## Rules that govern every decision
- Research before architecture: proven practice vs experimental vs vendor claim vs hypothesis — every consequential decision needs provenance + measurable objective + acceptance criteria.
- Learn from verified evidence; never copy competitor txs or treat correlation as superiority.
- Evolution cycle: observe→research→verify→benchmark→diagnose→prioritize→experiment→validate→integrate→measure→repeat.
- Autonomy: L1-3 automated; L4 (live/signing/contracts/security/financial) Commander-held. No weakening.
- Top-3 is a measurable objective, not assumed: defined category, defensible evidence, risk-adjusted realized net, execution reliability, conversion, predicted-vs-realized, consistency, security, evidence confidence. Claimed only when evidence supports it; disclose benchmarking limits.
- Audit before modify; preserve sound components; no duplicate subsystems; no fabricated results; live blocker register.
